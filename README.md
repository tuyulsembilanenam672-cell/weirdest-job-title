One job title, the weirdest we could think of, for the make.fun bounty "Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown":

> Lead Mourner for Retired Office Printers

A real-sounding job frame with one gloriously odd twist: someone is paid to grieve, professionally, for the office printers that no longer print. The program writes the title to `/output/entry.txt`.
